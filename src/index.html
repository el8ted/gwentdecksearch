        Help

        How to use it
        1. Pick a faction, then a leader. In a match, start with your opponent's.
        2. Type the cards you see into the Cards box (press /). Each one narrows the list.
        3. Most common shows the cards the remaining decks run; Least common shows rare surprises. Click one to add it.
        4. All lists decks with every card; Any lists decks with at least one, best matches first.
        5. Click a deck to see its cards. ↗ opens it on playgwent.com to import.
        6. Spare: room to upgrade a card. Over: costs have changed and the deck no longer fits.
        7. Clear all starts over; your browser's Back button undoes it.
        Search tips
        • Tags (#) such as Beast or Devotion, and authors (✎) by their playgwent name, narrow the list like cards.
        • Type - before a name, or click a pick you've added, to find decks without it. Excluded picks look faded and struck through.
        • Paste a playgwent.com deck or guide link to find that deck, with its guide and video. Back restores your search.
        • Names match in your language or in English, and accents don't matter: coen finds Coën.
        Similar decks ≈
        • Turn on the deck's own provision step to list it too, marked This deck. Dim common then fades what they all share, so the swaps stand out.
        • Use it to find a playable version of an Over deck, or variations of your own. ✕ on the Similar pill, or Back, returns to your search.
        Filters
        • Every option shows how many decks it would leave; a dimmed 0 means none.
        • Source: community guides on playgwent.com, or a creator's deck list.
        • Guide & video: ▶ Has video keeps decks with a gameplay video; a language keeps decks whose guide or video is in it.
        • Provisions: how far a deck is from today's limit, 150 plus the leader's bonus. Pick several steps to combine them.
        • Stratagem: players swap these freely, so the same list often appears under another one.
